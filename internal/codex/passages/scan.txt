The words of an auspex that print the omen a machine-spirit shows

The "scan" of a machine-spirit's auspex is spoken once; what it prints, its
whitespace trimmed and at most four kibibytes of it, is the omen read. The
aspects whose omen, rendered and trimmed, equals it are the aspects the
subject fits; a machine-spirit without "omens" must print aspect names, and
fits every one it prints.

A scan that falls, outlasts the auspex's patience, prints nothing or prints
an omen never declared leaves the subject mute. Beside "trials", the scan
reads and the trials only prove what was set. An auspex holding neither
"scan" nor "trials" is heresy.

The scan is spoken with the invoker's own authority unless the auspex is
itself exalted. It hears SERVITOR_RITE, the inscriptions and SERVITOR_ASPECT,
but never {{omen}}, for it reads the omen rather than setting one.

Kindred passages: auspex, trials, machine-spirit, omens, patience

Exempla:
  "auspex": {"scan": "cat /sys/devices/system/cpu/smt/control"}

  "auspex": {"scan": "makoctl mode", "patience": "1s"}
