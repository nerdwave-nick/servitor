The named deeds a rite of pattern Mark II speaks when its subjects change

Under "observances" a rite of aspects lists named deeds that change no
subject of their own: the transition of a screen, the litany a theme keeps
for its awakening, a missive to the desktop. Each observance bears a
"name", unique among the observances and bound by the rule of names, and a
"designation": an "incantation" speaking its "words", or a "litany"
reciting its "scroll" with its "offerings". It may bear a "tongue", a
"patience", plain "reversion" words, "exalted" and "relics", and the guards
"to" and "from".

An observance is spoken only when a subject that names it changes: as the
"introit" of subjects, once, before the first of them that changes; as the
"recessional" of subjects, once, after the last of them that changes. It is
spoken in its place, among the subjects being set, never deferred to the
end of the invocation. A subject passed over because it already bears its
omen calls upon no observance. One observance is named in one of the two
places, never both, which is heresy; an observance no subject names is never
spoken, an impurity.

The guards weigh each invocation: "to" admits it when it names the aspect
invoked, "from" when it names the former aspect — and an invocation whose
former aspect is unknown is admitted by no "from". An empty guard admits
nothing; a guard unwritten admits every aspect. The pre-flight examines
every observance, admitted or not, before anything is touched.

An observance is spoken as an incantation or litany is: in its tongue, else
the rite's, else the settings', else bash; in the rite's hall; within its
patience; hearing SERVITOR_ASPECT, SERVITOR_FORMER_ASPECT, SERVITOR_RITE and
the inscriptions, yet never SERVITOR_OMEN, for it sets no omen. Should it
fall, it fells the invocation as a subject does, and the reversion speaks
its reversion words, if it bears any, in its place among the restorations.
Exalted, it is spoken by the exalted servitor from the sanction of its
master.

Its words are written once for every aspect, never as an aspect map; words
that differ by aspect are written as several observances guarded by "to".
An edict bears no observances: its liturgy is spoken whole.

Kindred passages: introit, recessional, to, from, words, scroll, subjects,
reversion, exalted

Exempla:
  "observances": [
    {"name": "herald-silence", "designation": "incantation", "to": ["on"],
     "words": "notify-send -t 2000 'The heralds fall silent'"},
    {"name": "awaken", "designation": "litany",
     "scroll": "scripts/recite-hooks",
     "offerings": ["~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}"],
     "reversion": "test -z \"$SERVITOR_FORMER_ASPECT\" || scripts/recite-hooks ~/.config/nfluff/themes/\"$SERVITOR_FORMER_ASPECT\""}
  ]
