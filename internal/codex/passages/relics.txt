The scriptures exalted words read beside them, sealed into the sanction

Exalted speech is never spoken where its invoker keeps it. The exalted
servitor speaks it in a hall of its own, fresh for the invocation, so that
no hand but the machine's may alter what it reads between the sanction and
the speaking. Whatever the speech reads beside it — a list, a lesser scroll
it sources, the words of a tuning — must therefore be named under "relics".

"relics" is a list of paths, each reckoned from the hall where the rite's
scripture is kept and lying within it; a path that begins at the root, or
climbs out of that hall, is heresy. Their words are sealed into the sanction
beside the words, and the exalted servitor lays each under the same name in
its own hall before the speech begins, where "./<name>" finds it.

Only an exalted machine-spirit, an exalted observance and an exalted verse of
an edict bear relics; anywhere else they are heresy. The relics of a
machine-spirit serve its enact words and an auspex of its own marked
exalted. A relic that cannot be found is heresy too, denounced by the
inquisition. Exalted speech that names "./<name>", or sources a scripture,
not among its relics is an impurity.

Kindred passages: exalted, litany, incantation

Exempla:
  {"name": "governor", "designation": "litany", "exalted": true,
   "scroll": "scrolls/governor", "relics": ["scrolls/governor.conf"]}

  {"name": "tuning", "designation": "machine-spirit", "exalted": true,
   "auspex": {"scan": "cat /sys/kernel/tuning"},
   "enact": {"*": "source ./tuning.list && apply {{omen}}"},
   "relics": ["tuning.list"]}
