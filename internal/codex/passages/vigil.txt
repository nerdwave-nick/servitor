Keep vigil over the census, telling each rite as soon as it is known

The rune --vigil is given to the census. Without it the census is spoken
whole, once the last auspex has settled. With it the census keeps vigil:
it tells every rite at once, as far as it can be read without an auspex,
then tells again each rite whose auspex was still awaited, as soon as that
auspex settles. A lesser machine — a picker, a bar — may thus show every
rite at once and mend each as its auspex answers.

The vigil is spoken in binharic, with --binharic or without it: one object
upon each line of stdout, and every object names its kind under the key
"herald" ("rite", "lament" or "concluded"). Its order is fixed:

  first    one "rite" line for every rite, by name. A rite whose auspex is
           yet to answer bears "auspex": "awaited"; a rite without auspex,
           an edict and a heretical rite are told whole at once, and are
           never told again
  then     one further "rite" line for every awaited rite, in the order
           their auspexes settle, bearing "heeded" or "mute"
  last     exactly one "concluded", once every rite is settled

A "lament" line — a data-slate that cannot be read, naming its rite — may
come anywhere before the conclusion. A later "rite" line replaces the
earlier one of the same rite entirely; nothing is ever told in part. Once
"concluded" is told, the last "rite" line of every rite, its herald set
aside, is that rite's entry of `servitor census --binharic`, key for key
and value for value, auspex and canon among them.

The auspexes are awakened together, never more than sixteen at once, as
for the plain census. With --spare-auspex none is awakened: every rite is
told once, whole, bearing "forgone" where it keeps an auspex, and the
conclusion follows at once.

Should the reader of stdout depart before the conclusion, the vigil ends:
every auspex still labouring is slain with its whole process group, and
the servitor departs without a word upon stderr; its death-mark is then of
no meaning. A vigil invokes nothing, as the census never does.

Kindred passages: herald, lament, census, auspex, spare-auspex, binharic,
canon, data-slate

Exempla:
  servitor census --vigil

  servitor census --vigil |
    jq -c 'select(.herald == "rite") | {rite, aspect, auspex}'

  servitor census --vigil --spare-auspex
