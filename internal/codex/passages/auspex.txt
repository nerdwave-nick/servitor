A machine-spirit's own scanning words, telling which omen it shows

Some subjects leave no mark a vessel or a tether could reveal: a knob of the
kernel, a daemon awakened or slain, the mode a herald of the desktop keeps.
Such a subject is a machine-spirit, and its "auspex" is its own scanning
device: words spoken in its tongue that tell which of its omens it shows
now. The auspex is an object of up to four keys:

  "scan"      words spoken once, whose output, trimmed, is the omen read
  "trials"    one yes-or-no trial per omen, the death-mark 0 meaning yes;
              an omen without a trial is what remains when every trial
              fails
  "patience"  how long the auspex may labour, two seconds unless written;
              when it runs out, its whole process group is slain
  "exalted"   true when only the root may read the subject (see below)

At least one of "scan" and "trials" is demanded, and with both the scan
reads. An auspex that falls, outlasts its patience, prints nothing, prints
an omen never declared, or whose reading no omen fits is mute: it gives no
omen, and is never mistaken for one. A machine-spirit without an auspex
cannot be read: it is enacted on every invocation and never proven, which
the Inquisition notes. A sanctum, transcription or tether reads itself and
bears no auspex.

The auspex is spoken in the subject's tongue (else the rite's, else the
settings', else bash), from the hall of the rite's scripture, with
SERVITOR_RITE, the inscriptions of the data-slate as
SERVITOR_INSCRIPTION_<KEY>, and SERVITOR_ASPECT — the aspect invoked during
an invocation, empty when an augury or census asks outside of one; a trial
hears its own omen as {{omen}} and SERVITOR_OMEN. One augury awakens every
auspex of a rite at once, each under its own patience, and the census and
its vigil never more than sixteen of their processes at once — every trial
counting as one of its own.

An auspex itself marked "exalted": true reads what only the root may read: a
table of the machine's firewall, a scripture-file sealed 0600. It is allowed
upon an exalted subject alone, and its words, tongue, patience and relics
are part of the sanction. It is never spoken by an augury, a census, a
vigil, completion or foresight, which never ask a proof: the exalted servitor
alone speaks it, at invocation, and answers only which declared omens fit —
never the words printed. Outside an invocation its subject shows the omen
last recorded on the data-slate, stale, or none.

The augury, the census, the vigil and the cogitator awaken the auspexes;
completion never does, so that a Tab is answered at once, nor --spare-auspex,
which reads the recorded omens in their stead. What the auspexes of a rite
gave is told in the binharic of every augury and of every rite of the
census, under the key "auspex":

  ""         no machine-spirit of the rite keeps an auspex the augury may
             speak, or the rite is an edict, or heretical
  "forgone"  the auspexes were spared at the faithful's behest
             (--spare-auspex), or by completion
  "heeded"   every auspex spoken named an omen of the subject
  "mute"     at least one auspex was mute
  "awaited"  told only while the census keeps vigil (--vigil): an auspex
             has not yet settled, and the rite will be told again, heeded
             or mute, once it has; no augury and no census spoken whole
             ever bears it

The key is no omen; the omens themselves, and the standing judged from
them, are told apart (see omens).

In the elder pattern "Mark I" the auspex was the rite's own, one set of
words printing the aspect of the whole rite, short as the words alone or
long as {"rite": "<words>", "patience": "5s"}. Conversion makes it the scan
of the rite's only machine-spirit, and strikes it when there are several or
none (see conversion).

Kindred passages: scan, trials, machine-spirit, omens, standing, augury,
census, spare-auspex, vigil, patience, tongue, exalted

Exempla:
  "auspex": {"scan": "makoctl mode"}

  "auspex": {"trials": {"cosmos": "systemctl is-active -q scx-cosmos"},
             "patience": "1s"}

  // what only the root may read, spoken by the exalted servitor alone
  "auspex": {"scan": "nft list table inet gaming >/dev/null && echo gaming || echo plain",
             "exalted": true}

  servitor augury do-not-disturb | jq -r .auspex
