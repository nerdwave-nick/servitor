Perform a rite in reverent silence, withholding the tidings of triumph

The rune --silence (shorthand -s) is given to the ritual invoke. When the
invocation triumphs, the servitor withholds the summary it would otherwise
speak — the rite, the former and the new aspect, and every verse performed —
and says nothing at all; its exit of 0 alone bears witness.

Silence does not hide failure: should the pre-flight forbid the invocation or
a step fall, the lament is still spoken, with the fallen verse, its last
words and the outcome of every reversion. Neither does silence still the
vox-casts of the liturgy: those are heard upon the controlling terminal, or
sent to the desktop, as the rite commands. To still the desktop's vox, set
the key "vox" of the settings to "off".

Silence suits rites invoked from hotkeys, bars and other rites, where only
the exit and the vox-casts matter.

Kindred passages: invoke, vox, vox-cast, foresee

Exempla:
  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on --silence
  bindsym $mod+m exec servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on -s
