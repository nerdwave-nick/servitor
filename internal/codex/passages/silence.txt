Perform a rite in reverent silence, withholding the report of the invocation

The rune --silence (shorthand -s) is given to the ritual invoke. The
servitor then withholds the report it would otherwise tell — the header
with the rite, the former and the new aspect, the line of every verse
performed and the closing line of triumph. Upon a controlling terminal only
the lines the rite's own vox-casts ask for remain, told as ever:

  ❯ servitor invoke theme porpl --silence
    ⋯ Incense burns; the tether is complete.  (2/3)
  ✠ Binharic hymns resound: the aspect «porpl» is attained.

A rite without vox-casts says nothing at all; its exit of 0 alone bears
witness. Without a terminal the vox-casts go to the desktop, as the rite
commands, and nothing is printed.

Silence does not hide failure: should the pre-flight forbid the invocation or
a step fall, the lament is still spoken, with the fallen verse, its last
words and the outcome of every reversion. To still the desktop's vox, set
the key "vox" of the settings to "off".

Silence suits rites invoked from hotkeys, bars and other rites, where only
the exit and the vox-casts matter.

Kindred passages: invoke, vox, vox-cast, foresee

Exempla:
  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on --silence
  bindsym $mod+m exec servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on -s
