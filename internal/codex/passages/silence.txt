Perform a rite in reverent silence, withholding the report of the invocation

The rune --silence (shorthand -s) is given to the ritual invoke. The servitor
then withholds the report it would otherwise tell — the header with the rite,
the former and the new aspect (or an edict's name alone), the line of every
subject set, observance spoken or verse performed, and the closing line of
triumph. Upon a controlling terminal
only the lines the rite's own vox-casts ask for remain, told as ever:

  ❯ servitor invoke theme porpl --silence
    ⋯ Cogs turn: visage now bears the aspect «porpl».  (1/1)
  ✠ Every rune turned, every litany sung: the aspect «porpl» prevails.

The awaiting of dominion, told before the header, is withheld as well, and
so are the tidings that a rite's exalted words await the master's proof;
the intercessor still asks for that proof, once, upon the terminal. A
rite without vox-casts says nothing at all; its exit of 0 alone bears
witness. Without a terminal the vox-casts go to the desktop, as the rite
commands, and nothing is printed.

Silence does not hide failure: should the pre-flight forbid the invocation or
anything fall, the lament is still spoken, with what fell, its last
words and the outcome of every reversion. To still the desktop's vox, set
the key "vox" of the settings to "off".

Silence suits rites invoked from hotkeys, bars and other rites, where only
the exit and the vox-casts matter.

Kindred passages: invoke, vox, vox-cast, foresee, dominion, exalted, intercessor

Exempla:
  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on --silence
  bindsym $mod+m exec servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on -s
  bindsym Print exec servitor invoke screenshot -s
