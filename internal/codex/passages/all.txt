Abjure every sanction laid for you at once

The rune --all is given to the ritual abjure, in place of the name of a rite.
It withdraws every sanction laid for you, forsaken ones among them, with one
proof of the master; each sanction struck is told. When none is laid, the
abjuring is refused before any proof, its death-mark 2. --all beside the name
of a rite is refused alike.

Kindred passages: abjure, sanction

Exempla:
  servitor abjure --all
