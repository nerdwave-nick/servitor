The servitor's record of each rite's last invocation

For every rite it has invoked, the servitor keeps a data-slate: a small
scripture-file of binharic at

  $XDG_STATE_HOME/servitor/data-slates/<rite>.json

(beneath ~/.local/state when $XDG_STATE_HOME is unset). It is written by the
servitor alone, after each invocation it performs — never by a step of the
liturgy, never by hand — and it holds three things:

  aspect         the aspect the invocation left the rite in: the aspect
                 invoked after a triumph, the former aspect after a fall
  inscriptions   the inscriptions of that aspect, from runes and decrees
  last_rite      the verdict of the invocation and its hour, "at"

The data-slate of a rite of aspects of pattern Mark II holds two things
more, whatever the verdict of its invocation:

  boot           the boot of the machine the invocation was performed in,
                 as /proc/sys/kernel/random/boot_id names it
  omens          the omen every machine-spirit was known to show at the end
                 of the invocation, by subject: "omen", the declared omen
                 read, "at", the hour it was read, and "exalted": true when
                 the exalted servitor read it in the invoker's stead

A subject is known when the last thing done to it was a reading: the
reading before the invocation for a subject unchanged or never reached,
the proof of a subject set, or the proof of its restoration. A subject set
or restored without a proof — its proof fell, or it has no auspex — and a
mute one bear no entry, and whatever an earlier invocation recorded for
them is struck. Sanctums, transcriptions and tethers are read afresh in an
instant and are not recorded.

These recorded omens serve as stale omens where a live omen is not heard:
an exalted auspex, never spoken outside an invocation; an auspex spared by
--spare-auspex or by completion; and the first telling of a vigil, before
the auspexes answer. A recorded omen of another boot is never used, for
most of what a machine-spirit reads is lost when the machine is raised
anew, and neither is one the scripture no longer declares. An invocation
never trusts a recorded omen: it reads every subject live. The passage on
the omens tells all.

An edict stands in no aspect, so its data-slate holds only the inscriptions
and last_rite: the inscriptions it was proclaimed with after a triumph,
those recorded before after a fall.

The data-slate is the servitor's memory, not its eyes. It names the aspect
only when no omen can be read — a rite whose liturgy holds only incantations
and litanies, and no auspex. Where omens speak and agree on an aspect the
data-slate did not record, the rite is desecrated, and the augury names the
remembered aspect as "former". The inscriptions an augury speaks are those
of the data-slate; a rite never invoked through the servitor has none. For
an edict the data-slate is all there is: the verdict of its last rite alone
judges it dormant, proclaimed or corrupted.

An invocation refused in its pre-flight, or only foreseen, leaves the
data-slate untouched. A data-slate garbled beyond reading is denounced;
strike it, and the next invocation inscribes it anew. Inscriptions are kept
on the data-slate alone: the scripture of a rite is never rewritten by an
invocation.

Kindred passages: verdicts, inscriptions, standing, augury, chronicle,
omens, subjects, census, vigil

Exempla:
  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on --reason "gaming remnant"
  cat ~/.local/state/servitor/data-slates/mouse-autohide-toggle.json

  {"aspect": "on", "inscriptions": {"reason": "gaming remnant"},
   "last_rite": {"verdict": "triumph", "at": "2026-10-10T13:30:00Z"}}

  servitor augury mouse-autohide-toggle reason

  cat ~/.local/state/servitor/data-slates/gaming.json

  {"aspect": "on", "inscriptions": {},
   "last_rite": {"verdict": "triumph", "at": "2026-10-13T20:00:00Z"},
   "boot": "a74aeb41-cd68-4760-b796-138592d15e93",
   "omens": {"smt": {"omen": "off", "at": "2026-10-13T20:00:00Z"},
             "nft": {"omen": "gaming", "at": "2026-10-13T20:00:00Z",
                     "exalted": true}}}

  {"inscriptions": {"region": "full"},
   "last_rite": {"verdict": "triumph", "at": "2026-10-10T13:31:00Z"}}
