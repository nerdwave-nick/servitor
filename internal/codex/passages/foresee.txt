Divine what an invocation would do, without performing any of it

The rune --foresee (shorthand -f) is given to the ritual invoke. The
servitor then performs the whole pre-flight — illuminating every placeholder,
reading every tome, weighing every vessel, tether and tongue, resolving every
inscription — and reveals, verse by verse, what the invocation would do:
the changes to each vessel as a comparison of its lines, a seal that would
change, a vessel that would be consecrated or struck from the machine, each
tether that would be bound, rebound or unbound, and each incantation and
litany as it would be spoken, with its tongue, its patience and its
reversion. Should the pre-flight find heresy, the heresies are recited
instead, exactly as a true invocation would recite them.

Nothing is performed. No vessel or tether is touched, no command is spoken,
not even the rite's auspex is awakened; no vox-cast is sent, the data-slate
is not written and the chronicle is not added to. The former aspect shown is
read from the omens of the steps and the data-slate alone. An edict is
foreseen alike, its header naming the edict alone, for it has no aspect to
leave or to enter:

  +++ Foreseen: the edict screenshot +++

Foresight never exalts: no proof is asked and the exalted servitor is never
raised. The foresight of a rite with exalted verses first tells how their
plan for the aspect stands towards its sanction — sanctioned under its
sigil, or unsanctioned or lapsed, naming servitor sanction <rite> — and
marks each exalted verse ✠ after its name. A vessel of an exalted verse its
invoker may not read is foreseen by its owner, seal and size, and for a
transcription by the SHA-256 of the scripture it shall hold, with "contents
hidden — change unknown", never as undisturbed; the bound name of an exalted
tether its invoker may not behold, alike.

Foresight is heeded even of a servitor roused as root, which refuses every
true invocation; under sudo it foresees upon the Librarium and the vessels of
the one who spoke sudo, and writes nothing.

Foresee before every invocation you are not certain of, and before invoking
a rite whose scripture was newly amended.

Kindred passages: invoke, pre-flight, silence, auspex, chronicle, exalted,
sanction

Exempla:
  servitor invoke theme porpl --foresee
  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on -f --reason "gaming remnant"
  servitor invoke screenshot -f --region full
