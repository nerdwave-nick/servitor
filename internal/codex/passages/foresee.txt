Divine what an invocation would do, without performing any of it

The rune --foresee (shorthand -f) is given to the ritual invoke. The
servitor then performs the whole pre-flight — illuminating every placeholder,
reading every tome, weighing every vessel, tether and tongue, resolving every
inscription — and reveals what the invocation would do. Should the
pre-flight find heresy, the heresies are recited instead, exactly as a true
invocation would recite them.

A rite of aspects cannot tell what it would do without reading its
subjects, so its foresight awakens their auspexes, as an augury does, yet
speaks no enact word. It tells each subject on a line of its own, in
written order: a machine-spirit with the omen read and the omen commanded,
"(mute)" when its auspex named no omen and "(no auspex)" when it has none,
and whether it is unchanged or changing; beneath a changing one, the words
it would speak, or for a sanctum and transcription the changes to the vessel
as a comparison of its lines, a seal that would change, a vessel that would
be consecrated or struck, and for a tether how it would be bound, rebound or
unbound. Each observance that would be spoken stands in its place among
them, with its tongue, its patience and its reversion:

  +++ Foreseen: the rite theme, (dormant) → porpl +++
  observance veil · incantation
    spoken in the tongue bash, with the patience of 30s: bash -c "niri msg …"
  subject visage · tether ~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme: changing
    the tether is bound to ~/.config/nfluff/themes/porpl (it led to nothing)
  observance awaken · litany
    recited by its own shebang, with the patience of 1m0s: …/recite-hooks …
  Nothing was performed.

An edict is foreseen verse by verse, its header naming the edict alone, for
it has no aspect to leave or to enter:

  +++ Foreseen: the edict screenshot +++

Nothing is performed. No vessel or tether is touched, no enact word,
observance or verse is spoken, no vox-cast is sent, the data-slate is not
written and the chronicle is not added to. Scripture of pattern Mark I is
foreseen converted in memory alone, and is not written.

Foresight never exalts: no proof is asked and the exalted servitor is never
raised. The foresight of a rite with exalted subjects, observances or verses
first tells how their plan for the aspect stands towards its sanction —
sanctioned under its sigil, or unsanctioned or lapsed, naming servitor
sanction <rite> — and marks each exalted one ✠ after its name. An auspex
itself marked exalted is never spoken: its subject tells the omen the
data-slate last recorded in this boot, "(stale, read <hour>)", and is
planned from it, or "(read at invocation)" when none serves, and is planned
changing. A vessel of an exalted subject its invoker may not read is
foreseen by its owner, seal and size, and for a transcription by the
SHA-256 of the scripture it shall hold, with "contents hidden — change
unknown", never as undisturbed; the bound name of an exalted tether its
invoker may not behold, alike.

Foresight is heeded even of a servitor roused as root, which refuses every
true invocation; under sudo it foresees upon the Librarium and the vessels of
the one who spoke sudo, and writes nothing.

Foresee before every invocation you are not certain of, and before invoking
a rite whose scripture was newly amended.

Kindred passages: invoke, pre-flight, silence, auspex, subjects,
observances, chronicle, exalted, sanction

Exempla:
  servitor invoke theme porpl --foresee
  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on -f --reason "gaming remnant"
  servitor invoke screenshot -f --region whole

  subject smt · machine-spirit ✠: "on" → "off", changing
    spoken in the tongue bash, with the patience of 30s: bash -c "echo off > …"
  subject nft · machine-spirit ✠: (read at invocation) → "gaming", changing
