The rights of reading, writing and execution a vessel is to bear

Every scripture-file of the machine bears a seal: the rights it grants its
owner, its kin and all others to read it, write it and execute it, written
as the old cogitators write them — three or four octal numerals, such as
"0644" or "0755". The key "seal" belongs to the transcription subject and
decrees the seal its vessel shall bear.

Without a seal, a vessel the transcription brings into being bears 0644, and
a vessel that already stands keeps the seal it bore. With a seal, the vessel
takes it whether it is newly made or already stood. A seal is one value for
every aspect, written as a string; anything but three or four octal numerals
is heresy. Seals are never illuminated.

An exalted transcription heeds its seal alike: the vessel it brings into
being belongs to the root and bears the seal, else 0644, and a vessel that
stands keeps its owner, and its own seal unless one is decreed. The seal is
part of the sanction, so writing another lapses it.

Sanctums bear no "seal": they keep the seal of the vessel they dwell in, and
a vessel a sanctum consecrates bears 0644.

Should the invocation fall, the reversion restores the former seal along
with the former scripture.

Kindred passages: transcription, vessel, consecrate
