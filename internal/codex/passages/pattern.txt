The Mark of the form in which a scripture is written

Every scripture of the Librarium — each rite in rites/ and the settings in
servitor.json — must declare at its head the Mark of the form it is written
in. The key is "pattern", and its value is the word "Mark" followed by
numerals of the old tongue: "Mark I", "Mark II", "Mark IV" and so forth. This
servitor was forged to read pattern "Mark I", and that Mark alone.

The pattern is examined before anything else. A scripture that bears no
pattern, a pattern that is no Mark at all, or a Mark this servitor does not
know is heresy — and it is then the only heresy denounced, for the servitor
will not guess at the meaning of a form it was never taught, nor heap
complaints about unknown keys upon scripture of another age. Such a rite
stands heretical; such settings are not heeded, and the servitor keeps to its
defaults.

There is no conversion of elder scripture, and no form is read implicitly:
whoever keeps rites written for an earlier servitor must rewrite them in
pattern Mark I by hand, and the inquisition will denounce every one that is
left unrewritten.

Kindred passages: rite, librarium, inquisition, heresy

Exempla:
  {
    "pattern": "Mark I",
    "aspects": ["on", "off"],
    "liturgy": [{"incantation": "makoctl mode -t do-not-disturb"}]
  }

  // servitor.json, the settings beside the rites
  {"pattern": "Mark I", "tongue": "bash", "patience": "30s"}
