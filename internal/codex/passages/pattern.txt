The Mark of the form in which a scripture is written

Every scripture of the Librarium — each rite in rites/ and the settings in
servitor.json — must declare at its head the Mark of the form it is written
in. The key is "pattern", and its value is the word "Mark" followed by
numerals of the old tongue: "Mark I", "Mark II", "Mark IV" and so forth.

This servitor writes and performs pattern "Mark II". A rite of Mark II is
either a rite of aspects, which declares its "subjects" — each read and set by
itself — and the "observances" spoken when they change, or an edict, which
keeps a "liturgy" of incantations and litanies in the designation form. The
settings of Mark II keep the keys they always had.

The elder pattern "Mark I", whose rites performed one liturgy of steps, is
still read, but never kept: the first ritual that reads pure scripture of
Mark I and may write converts it to Mark II, once, keeps the elder scripture
in the hall .mark-i beside it and tells what changed. Rituals that only read
convert it in their memory alone. Scripture of Mark I that holds heresy is
not converted; it stands heretical until its heresies are purged. The
passages on conversion and mark-i tell all.

The pattern is examined before anything else. A scripture that bears no
pattern, a pattern that is no Mark at all, or a Mark this servitor does not
know is heresy — and it is then the only heresy denounced, for the servitor
will not guess at the meaning of a form it was never taught, nor heap
complaints about unknown keys upon scripture of another age. Such a rite
stands heretical; such settings are not heeded, and the servitor keeps to its
defaults. Scripture of the eldest form, which knew no pattern at all, is heresy,
and nothing of it is converted: write it anew.

Kindred passages: rite, conversion, mark-i, subjects, edict, librarium,
inquisition, heresy

Exempla:
  {
    "pattern": "Mark II",
    "aspects": ["on", "off"],
    "subjects": [
      {"name": "mako", "designation": "machine-spirit",
       "omens": {"on": "do-not-disturb", "*": "default"},
       "auspex": {"scan": "makoctl mode"},
       "enact": {"*": "makoctl mode -s \"$SERVITOR_OMEN\""}}
    ]
  }

  // servitor.json, the settings beside the rites
  {"pattern": "Mark II", "tongue": "bash", "patience": "30s"}
