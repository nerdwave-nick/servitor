Scripture drawn from another scripture-file rather than written inline

A tome lets a sanctum or transcription draw its scripture from a separate
scripture-file, so that long or intricate scripture need not be pressed into
a JSON string. It is written as an object in place of the scripture:

  {"tome": "<path>", "illuminate": true}

"tome" names the scripture-file; "illuminate" (false unless written)
decrees whether its placeholders are filled. The path may itself speak
placeholders — {{aspect}} chooses one tome per aspect — and a path not
rooted at "/" is reckoned from the rites/ hall of the Librarium, where the
rite's own scripture is kept; "~" and $NAMES are expanded.

The tome is read afresh in every pre-flight, so amending the tome amends the
next invocation without touching the rite. A tome that cannot be opened is
heresy in the pre-flight, and nothing is performed; the inquisition opens
every tome as well and denounces the missing. A tome is set down verbatim
unless illuminated; an illuminated tome with an unknown placeholder is
heresy located at its own line and column.

The augury reads tomes too, to judge which aspect a vessel holds; changing a
tome after an invocation makes the vessel no longer match its aspect.

Kindred passages: scripture, illuminate, placeholders, sanctum, transcription

Exempla:
  {"transcription": "~/.config/alacritty/colors.toml",
   "scripture": {"tome": "colors/{{aspect}}.toml"}}

  {"sanctum": "~/.config/niri/config.kdl",
   "scripture": {"on": {"tome": "niri/gaming.kdl", "illuminate": true},
                 "off": ""}}
