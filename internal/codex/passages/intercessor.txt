Who vouches for the master before an exalted servitor is raised, written in the settings

An exalted verse is performed with the machine's own authority, and the
machine lends it only to its master proven. The intercessor is the one who
asks for that proof, once for every invocation holding exalted verses. In the
settings, servitor.json beside the rites of the Librarium, "intercessor"
names it:

  "auto"    the servitor chooses by whether a terminal attends the
            invocation: sudo, else run0, else pkexec at a terminal; run0,
            else pkexec without one (this is the default)
  "sudo"    sudo alone
  "run0"    run0 alone
  "pkexec"  pkexec alone

An intercessor named but missing from the machine refuses the invocation.
Any other value is heresy, denounced by the inquisition with its place; doas
is never called upon. No rune and no variable of the environment outranks
the settings here.

Kindred passages: exalted, invoke, librarium

Exempla:
  // servitor.json
  {"pattern": "Mark I", "intercessor": "run0"}
