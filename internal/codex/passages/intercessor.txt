Who vouches for the master before an exalted servitor is raised, written in the settings

An exalted verse is performed with the machine's own authority, and the
machine lends it only to its master proven. The intercessor is the one who
asks for that proof, once for every invocation holding exalted verses. In the
settings, servitor.json beside the rites of the Librarium, "intercessor"
names it:

  "auto"    the servitor chooses by whether a terminal attends the
            invocation: sudo, else run0, else pkexec at a terminal; run0,
            else pkexec without one (this is the default)
  "sudo"    sudo alone
  "run0"    run0 alone
  "pkexec"  pkexec alone

An intercessor named but missing from the PATH refuses the invocation with
the death-mark 2, and no other is called upon in its stead. Any other value
is heresy, denounced by the inquisition with its place; doas is never called
upon. No rune and no variable of the environment outranks the settings here.
Without a terminal, sudo asks for the master's proof only through a helper
its master laid outside the servitor, so "auto" passes it by.

The intercessor always raises the servitor installed at /usr/bin/servitor,
never the servitor that asks, and the servitor calls upon none unless that
one stands where the root alone may write: owned by the root, written by
none but the root, it and every hall above it. Otherwise the invocation is
refused with the death-mark 2, before any proof is asked; install the
servitor anew from its package (makepkg -si). No settings spare this
judgement. Each intercessor is called upon with these words alone, the
sigil cut to its first twelve letters, or eight in the name of run0's unit:

  sudo -k -p "servitor: authenticate to perform <rite> (sigil <sigil>): " \
    -- /usr/bin/servitor __exalt <rite> <sigil> [<aspect>]
  run0 --unit=servitor-<rite>-<sigil8> --pipe --chdir=/ \
    /usr/bin/servitor __exalt <rite> <sigil> [<aspect>]
  pkexec /usr/bin/servitor __exalt <rite> <sigil> [<aspect>]

sudo forgets every proof given before (-k), so that each invocation asks
anew; pkexec asks through the stock action of the machine.

Kindred passages: exalted, exalted-servitor, invoke, librarium

Exempla:
  // servitor.json
  {"pattern": "Mark I", "intercessor": "run0"}
