The name of a subject or an observance of pattern Mark II

Every subject and every observance of a rite of pattern "Mark II" bears a
"name". By it the tidings tell which subject changed or fell, the chronicle
records where an invocation fell, and a subject names the observances it
calls upon as its introit or recessional.

A name must begin with a letter or digit and continue only with letters,
digits, ".", "_" or "-", as the name of an aspect does. No two subjects of a
rite may bear one name, nor may two observances; a subject and an observance
may share one, for they are never mistaken for each other. A subject or
observance without a name is heresy.

The entries of an edict's liturgy bear no name: they are named by their
verse, counted from one, as the steps of a liturgy always were.

Kindred passages: subjects, observances, introit, recessional, verse

Exempla:
  {"name": "split-lock", "designation": "machine-spirit", ...}

  {"name": "herald-silence", "designation": "incantation", ...}
