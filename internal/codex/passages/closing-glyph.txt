The mark that seals a sanctum's markers where remarks must be closed

Some tongues enclose their remarks between an opening and a closing mark —
CSS within "/*" and "*/", HTML and XML within "<!--" and "-->". For such a
vessel the closing glyph follows the final "+++" of each marker, so that the
remark is sealed and the vessel's machine-spirit does not swallow the
scripture after it:

  /* +++ begin of sanctum palette -- aspect|dark +++ */
  /* +++ end of sanctum palette +++ */

When neither "glyph" nor "closing-glyph" is written, the closing glyph is
divined from the vessel's extension together with the glyph: "*/" for .css,
"-->" for .html, .xml, .svg and .md, and none for every other tongue. When
"closing-glyph" alone is written, the glyph is still divined; when "glyph" is
written alone, there is no closing glyph at all.

A closing glyph must be a single line, and the ward may not contain it, for
it would end the marker in the ward's midst. It is never illuminated.

Kindred passages: glyph, sanctum, ward, vessel

Exempla:
  {"sanctum": "~/.local/share/startpage/index.htm",
   "glyph": "<!--", "closing-glyph": "-->",
   "scripture": {"dark": "<body class=\"night\">", "light": "<body>"}}
