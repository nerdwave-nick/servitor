The undoing of a fallen invocation, and the words that undo a command

Should a step fall — its death-mark not 0, its patience run out, or the
invocation halted at its master's command — the servitor does not leave the
machine half-changed. It reverts: every step already performed is undone in
reverse order, from the fallen step back to the first, so that the machine
returns to its former aspect.

Sanctums, transcriptions and tethers revert themselves, for the servitor
captured what stood before it changed them: a sanctum and a transcribed
vessel are written back as they were (a vessel the step consecrated is
removed again), and a tether is rebound to its former anchor, or unbound.

Incantations and litanies cannot be undone by the servitor alone; it does
not know what their words did. Under "reversion" such a step carries the
words that undo it — one line, spoken in the step's tongue, under the
step's patience, in the rite's hall, with the same environment as the step.
They may differ per aspect as an aspect map, and {{former}} (and
SERVITOR_FORMER_ASPECT) name the aspect the machine is to return to. The
reversion of the fallen step itself is spoken too, for it may have done half
its work. A step without reversion reverts nothing.

A reversion that falls does not stop the others; every one is attempted and
its outcome told. When all succeed the verdict is reverted; when any falls,
the verdict is faltered, and the rite will likely show corrupted. A halt
from the terminal never halts the reversions. When foreseeing, the reversion
each step would speak is shown.

Kindred passages: verdicts, incantation, litany, placeholders, chronicle,
invoke

Exempla:
  {"incantation": "systemctl --user stop wlsunset",
   "reversion": "systemctl --user start wlsunset"}

  // aspects "sharp" and "soft": only leaving "sharp" needs undoing
  {"incantation": {"sharp": "hyprctl keyword decoration:rounding 0",
                   "soft": "hyprctl keyword decoration:rounding 12"},
   "reversion": {"sharp": "hyprctl reload", "*": ""}}

  {"litany": "hooks/apply-theme", "offerings": ["{{aspect}}"],
   "reversion": "hooks/apply-theme \"$SERVITOR_FORMER_ASPECT\""}
