Whether vox-casts reach the desktop, written in the settings

The vox is the servitor's channel to the desktop. In the settings,
servitor.json beside the rites of the Librarium, "vox" decides whether the
vox-casts of an invocation roused without a terminal are sent there:

  "notify-send"  every vox-cast becomes one desktop missive through the
                 program notify-send, replaced in place by the next
                 (this is the default)
  "off"          the desktop hears nothing

The vox governs the desktop alone: an invocation spoken at a terminal still
tells its vox-casts in its report, whatever the vox commands. Should notify-send be
missing from the machine, the servitor stays silent rather than fail. Any
other value is heresy, denounced by the inquisition with its place.

Kindred passages: vox-cast, invoke, librarium

Exempla:
  // servitor.json
  {"pattern": "Mark I", "vox": "off"}
