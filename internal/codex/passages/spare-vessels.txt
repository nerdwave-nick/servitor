Judge the scriptures alone, sparing the machine they act upon

The rune --spare-vessels is given to the Inquisition. Without it, the
Inquisition judges not only the scriptures of the rites and the settings but
also the machine their liturgies act upon: it seeks out tongues spoken
nowhere, vessels missing without "consecrate", anchors missing for some
aspect, tainted sanctums and desecrated rites — and to know desecration it
reads every omen and awakens every auspex.

With the rune, the Inquisition weighs the scriptures alone: their form,
keys, aspects, aspect maps, placeholders, inscriptions, tomes and wards, and
the settings. No vessel is read and no auspex awakened, so the judgement is
swift and the same upon every machine — fit for scriptures kept in a
repository and judged far from the machine they will serve.

Kindred passages: inquisition, heresy, vessel, auspex

Exempla:
  servitor inquisition --spare-vessels
  servitor --librarium ./servitor inquisition --spare-vessels --binharic
