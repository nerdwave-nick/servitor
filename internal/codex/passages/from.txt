The aspects an observance is spoken out of

Under "from" an observance lists the declared aspects it is spoken out of:
it is spoken only when the machine stood, before the invocation, in one of
them. The former aspect is the one the subjects agreed upon when read, or
the data-slate's among several; when it cannot be told, no observance
guarded by "from" is spoken. Without "from" an observance is spoken out of
every aspect.

Every aspect named must be declared by the rite, else heresy. "to" and
"from" may guard one observance together; both must admit the invocation.

Kindred passages: to, observances, introit, recessional, data-slate
