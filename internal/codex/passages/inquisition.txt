Summon the Inquisition to denounce heresy in rites and vessels

The Inquisition examines every rite of the Librarium — or only the rites
named to it — together with the settings and the machine their liturgies act
upon, and denounces all it finds. Each denunciation bears its place as
scripture:line:column, so that the faithful may go straight to the offending
word, and a verbose account of the sin and its penance. Nothing is changed by
the Inquisition.

Roused as root by sudo, the Inquisition examines the invoker's Librarium and
the vessels of the invoker's home, never root's; the passage on the Librarium
tells all.

Heresies must be purged, for a heretical rite cannot be invoked: malformed
scripture, unknown keys, a missing or foreign pattern, aspects empty, repeated
or unworthily named, a rite bearing both "aspects" and "edict": true or
neither, aspect maps naming aspects never declared, an aspect left without a
value and without "*", an edict holding a sanctum, transcription, tether,
auspex, aspect map, {{aspect}} or {{former}}, unknown placeholders and
inscriptions never declared, missing tomes, a null scripture in a sanctum, a
vox-cast that is neither "progress" nor "success", a ward claimed twice in one
vessel, a rite recorded in two scriptures, heretical settings, sanctums
whose markers are broken, exalted speech in a tongue other than bash, zsh,
fish, sh or dash (by the program it truly is), and an exalted tether whose
anchor lies beneath a hall its invoker may write (spared by
--spare-vessels).

Impurities are noted but forgiven: tongues spoken nowhere on this machine,
vessels missing without "consecrate", anchors missing for some aspect, the
scroll of an edict's litany that stands nowhere, mandatory inscriptions
without a decree for some aspect, a "success" vox-cast that is not the last
step, tainted sanctums, desecrated rites, an exalted rite that stands
unsanctioned or whose sanction lapsed, a forsaken sanction, found at the
head of its sanction, and, while a rite examined holds exalted verses, the
servitor installed at /usr/bin/servitor missing or not standing where the
root alone may write, found at /usr/bin/servitor itself.

A rite of pattern "Mark II" is weighed subject by subject and observance by
observance, as a liturgy of Mark I is weighed verse by verse, and each finding
names the subject or observance it belongs to. Beyond the heresies of its
scripture, its impurities are omens alike for every aspect, an observance no
subject names in its "introit" or "recessional", a machine-spirit without an
auspex (noted more sharply when it is exalted, naming an auspex marked
"exalted": true as the remedy), the tongue of a machine-spirit's auspex spoken
nowhere, noted at the auspex itself; and every judgement of Mark I that still
applies, heresy or impurity: the tomes of sanctum and transcription subjects,
vessels missing without "consecrate", anchors of tether subjects missing for
some aspect, the tongues of machine-spirits and observances, the relics and
tongues of exalted subjects and observances, the words the scrolls of
observances speak, decrees, taint and desecration.

To weigh the vessels, anchors and desecration, the Inquisition reads the
omens of every rite and awakens its auspex. The machine is weighed for every
aspect of a rite, and once for an edict, which bears none. The rune
--spare-vessels spares the machine this scrutiny and judges the scriptures
alone.

Spoken plainly, every finding stands on a line of its own and the sum of the
Inquisition follows. With --binharic the findings are a list for other
machines, each bearing scripture, line, column, rite (absent for the settings
and the Librarium itself), judgement ("heresy" or "impurity") and
denunciation.

The Inquisition exits 1 when any heresy was found, and 0 when only
impurities, or nothing at all, were found.

Kindred passages: heresy, spare-vessels, binharic, standing, census, pattern,
sanction

Exempla:
  servitor inquisition
  servitor inquisition theme mouse-autohide-toggle --spare-vessels
  servitor inquisition --binharic |
    jq -r '.[] | select(.judgement == "heresy") | .denunciation'
