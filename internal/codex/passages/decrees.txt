The word each aspect inscribes when the invocation gives none

An inscription's "decrees" name, per aspect, the word inscribed when an
invocation gives no rune for it. They are written as an aspect map: a single
word serves every aspect, or an object names one word per aspect, with "*"
serving every aspect not named. Unlike the aspect maps of the steps, decrees
may leave aspects without a word; such an invocation is simply uninscribed,
unless the inscription is mandatory.

A rune given to the invocation outranks the decree; a rune given the empty
word clears it. Decrees are never illuminated: placeholders written in them
are kept as written.

Completion offers the decreed words when the rune of the inscription is
written, each with the aspects that decree it, and the inquisition notes as
an impurity a mandatory inscription that some aspect does not decree.

Kindred passages: inscriptions, mandatory, aspect-maps, invoke

Exempla:
  "inscriptions": {
    "mode": {"decrees": {"on": "auto-hide", "off": "always-visible"}},
    "origin": {"decrees": "the hotkey"},
    "reason": {"decrees": {"porpl": "a royal mood", "*": ""}}
  }

  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on                  # mode=auto-hide
  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on --mode sentinel  # the rune outranks
