The place of a step within its liturgy, counted from one

The steps of a liturgy are its verses, and each is named by its place: the
first step written is verse 1, the next verse 2, and so on to the last,
vox-casts included. Wherever the servitor speaks of one step, it names the
verse and then the step by its own key and what that key holds — never by a
bare number, but as the liturgy itself would say it.

In the binharic of an augury, every omen and every taint bears "verse" beside
the step's own key: {"verse": 2, "tether": "…"}. The chronicle names the step
that fell as "fell_at": {"verse": 3, "incantation": "…"}, and each reversion
by its verse and verdict. On the terminal, foresight, summaries and laments
speak "verse 1 · sanctum ~/.config/niri/util.kdl".

The heresies of a step are found by line and column within the scripture,
not by verse; the verse names the step in what the servitor performed or
observed. The vox-casts of progress tell "step x / n", counting only the true
steps, and their tidings may name the verse just performed as {{step}}.

Kindred passages: liturgy, omens, chronicle, verdicts, foresee

Exempla:
  servitor invoke theme porpl --foresee

  verse 1 · vox-cast progress
  verse 2 · tether ~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme
  verse 3 · incantation source ~/.config/nfluff/themes/porpl/hooks

  {"verse": 2, "tether": "~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme",
   "aspect": "porpl"}
