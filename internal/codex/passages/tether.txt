A step that binds a name in the machine to an anchor elsewhere

Know that a tether is a binding of the machine's own making: a name upon the
filesystem that leads whoever reads it onward to its anchor, as a vox-relay
leads a summons to a distant shrine. The tether step binds such a name to the
anchor its aspect demands, rebinds it when another aspect is invoked, and
unbinds it when the anchor is written null.

The step's own key holds the bound name; "anchor" holds where it leads, and
may differ for every aspect as an aspect map. Both may speak placeholders, so
one anchor may serve every aspect through {{aspect}}. A leading "~" and the
$NAMES of the environment are expanded in both, and a path that does not
begin at the root is reckoned from the hall where the rite's scripture is
kept (the rites/ hall of the Librarium); the tether is bound to the full path
so reckoned.

The servitor rebinds only a tether. Should a scripture-file already stand
where the name is to be bound, the pre-flight refuses, for the servitor keeps
no copy of what it casts down — unless the step burns with "zeal", and then
the scripture-file gives way. A hall standing there never gives way, zeal or
no zeal. Missing halls above the name are never raised; the pre-flight
denounces them.

A tether yields an omen: the anchor it leads to is compared with the anchor
each aspect would render, and the aspect whose anchor it matches is the aspect
it bears witness to. The inquisition notes as an impurity an anchor that
stands nowhere for some aspect.

Should the invocation fall, the reversion restores the binding exactly as it
was found — rebound to its former anchor, or unbound if none stood before.

Kindred passages: anchor, zeal, omens, aspect-maps, placeholders

Exempla:
  {"tether": "~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme",
   "anchor": "~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}"}

  {"tether": "~/.config/foot/colors.ini",
   "anchor": {"dark": "~/.config/foot/night.ini", "*": null}}
