A subject that binds a name in the machine to an anchor elsewhere

Know that a tether is a binding of the machine's own making: a name upon the
filesystem that leads whoever reads it onward to its anchor, as a vox-relay
leads a summons to a distant shrine. A subject designated "tether" binds
such a name to the anchor its aspect demands, rebinds it when another aspect
is invoked, and unbinds it when the anchor is written null.

The key "tether" holds the bound name; "omens" holds where it leads in each
aspect, keyed by aspect with "*" for every other, and within an omen
{{aspect}} names the aspect whose omen it is. A leading "~" and the $NAMES
of the environment are expanded in both, and a path that does not begin at
the root is reckoned from the rites/ hall of the Librarium.

The servitor rebinds only a tether. Should a scripture-file already stand
where the name is to be bound, the pre-flight refuses — unless the subject
burns with "zeal", and then the scripture-file gives way. A hall never gives
way, and missing halls above the name are never raised.

A tether yields an omen: every aspect whose rendered anchor it leads to, or
every aspect whose omen is null when the name is unbound. At invocation it
is passed over when it already leads to the anchor of the aspect invoked.
The inquisition notes as an impurity an anchor that stands nowhere for some
aspect. Should the invocation fall, the binding is restored exactly as it
was found.

Exempla:
  {"name": "visage", "designation": "tether",
   "tether": "~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme",
   "omens": {"*": "~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}"}}

  {"name": "colors", "designation": "tether",
   "tether": "~/.config/foot/colors.ini",
   "omens": {"dark": "~/.config/foot/night.ini", "*": null}}

Exalted ("exalted": true), the tether is bound by the exalted servitor from
the sanction of its master: its name must begin at the root, beneath none of
/proc, /sys and /dev, and its anchor must be reached through halls the root
alone may write — a tether from /etc into the invoker's home is heresy, and
an exalted transcription is the remedy. A name its invoker may not behold is
decided at invocation by the exalted servitor, and foreseen with its change
unknown.

An edict may bind no tether, for it bears no aspect.

Kindred passages: anchor, zeal, omens, aspect-maps, placeholders, subjects,
designation, edict, exalted
