The ordered steps a rite performs, one after another as written

The "liturgy" of a rite is a list of steps, and every step is one object
named by its own key: "sanctum", "transcription", "tether", "incantation",
"litany" or "vox-cast". An object bearing none of these keys, or more than
one, is heresy, and so is any key the step's kind does not know.

At invocation the pre-flight first examines every step without touching
anything; only then are the steps performed, in the order written, never in
parallel and never reordered. Should one fall, the steps already performed
are reverted in reverse order, the fallen one included, and the machine
returns to its former aspect.

Each step is named by its verse, its place in the liturgy counted from one;
the foresight, the omens of an augury, the chronicle and the laments of the
servitor speak of "verse 2 · tether …". Vox-casts count among the verses but
not among the real steps of the "step x / n" that progress vox-casts tell.

Sanctums, transcriptions and tethers yield omens of the aspect the machine
stands in; incantations, litanies and vox-casts yield none.

Kindred passages: sanctum, transcription, tether, incantation, litany,
vox-cast, verse, pre-flight, reversion

Exemplum:
  "liturgy": [
    {"vox-cast": "progress"},
    {"tether": "~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme",
     "anchor": "~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}"},
    {"litany": "scripts/change-theme",
     "offerings": ["~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}"]},
    {"incantation": "makoctl reload", "reversion": "makoctl reload"},
    {"vox-cast": "success"}
  ]
