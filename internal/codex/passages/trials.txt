The words of an auspex that try, one omen each, which one a subject shows

Under "trials" a machine-spirit's auspex holds one trial per omen, keyed by
a declared omen or "*". Every trial is spoken at once under the auspex's one
patience; an omen fits when its trial ends with the death-mark 0. When no
trial succeeds, the omens bearing no trial fit, for they are what remains.
Each trial hears its own omen as {{omen}} and SERVITOR_OMEN.

A trial that outlasts the patience, cannot be spoken or is slain mutes the
whole auspex, as does a reading that no omen fits. A key naming no declared
omen is heresy. Not every omen needs a trial.

Beside a "scan", the trials are not spoken while reading: they only prove,
after a subject is set, that the omen invoked now shows.

Kindred passages: auspex, scan, machine-spirit, omens, death-marks

Exempla:
  "auspex": {"trials": {"cosmos": "systemctl is-active -q scx-cosmos"},
             "patience": "1s"}
