Leave for a sanctum to bring its missing vessel into being

A sanctum is meant to dwell within a vessel kept by other hands, and so by
default it refuses to conjure one: when the vessel named by the sanctum does
not stand, the pre-flight halts the invocation with heresy and nothing is
performed. Write "consecrate": true upon the sanctum, and a missing vessel is
instead brought into being, holding nothing but the sanctum itself.

A consecrated vessel bears the seal 0644. The hall that is to hold it must
already stand: consecration brings forth vessels, never halls.

Should the invocation fall after the vessel was consecrated, the reversion
strikes the vessel again, so the machine is as it was found.

The inquisition, when it examines the machine, notes as impurity a vessel
that is missing although its sanctum may not consecrate it, for every
invocation of that rite would be refused. With "consecrate": true, the
absent vessel is forgiven.

Transcriptions need no consecration: they write whole vessels and bring
them into being as a matter of course. "consecrate" belongs to the sanctum
alone, and holds one truth for every aspect.

Kindred passages: sanctum, vessel, pre-flight, inquisition

Exempla:
  {"sanctum": "~/.config/niri/overrides.kdl", "consecrate": true,
   "scripture": {"on": "prefer-no-csd", "off": ""}}
