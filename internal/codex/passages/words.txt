The words an observance or an entry of an edict speaks

Under "words" an observance or an edict's entry designated "incantation"
holds the line it speaks in its tongue, in the rite's hall, anointed with
SERVITOR_RITE, the inscriptions and, for a rite of aspects, SERVITOR_ASPECT
and SERVITOR_FORMER_ASPECT. A death-mark other than 0 makes it fall.

The words are written once for every aspect, never as an aspect map, and
may not be empty. They may speak every placeholder of a rite's words save
{{omen}}; an edict's words speak neither {{aspect}} nor {{former}}. Exalted
words are fixed at their sanction.

Kindred passages: incantation, observances, edict, tongue, patience,
reversion
