A scripture-file of the machine that a rite writes into

A vessel is any scripture-file of the machine that a rite reshapes: a sanctum
rewrites only its warded region within the vessel, a transcription writes
the whole vessel anew or casts it out. The step's own key holds the vessel's
path, and the path is one for every aspect, for omens and markers must know
where to look.

A leading "~" and the $NAMES of the environment are expanded in the path,
and placeholders are illuminated; a path that does not begin at the root is
reckoned from the hall where the rite's scripture is kept (the rites/ hall of
the Librarium). Write full paths, or paths from "~", unless the vessel truly
dwells beside the rite.

A vessel reached through a tether is followed: the scripture-file at the far
end is rewritten, and the tether itself remains as it was. Every rewriting is
whole or nothing — the new scripture is inscribed beside the vessel and then
set in its place at once, so no reader ever sees it half-written. The
vessel's seal is kept, and a vessel the servitor brings into being receives
the seal 0644 — unless a transcription names a "seal" of its own.

The servitor never raises missing halls above a vessel: the pre-flight
denounces them, and nothing is performed. A sanctum whose vessel is absent
consecrates it only when "consecrate" is true; otherwise the pre-flight
refuses, and the inquisition notes the missing vessel as an impurity. A
transcription replaces a vessel only when it holds the scripture of one of
the rite's aspects — or when the step burns with zeal. No copy of a vessel is
kept beyond the reversion of the invocation itself.

Kindred passages: sanctum, transcription, consecrate, seal, zeal, tether

Exempla:
  {"sanctum": "~/.config/niri/util.kdl",
   "scripture": {"on": "cursor {\n    hide-after-inactive-ms 400\n}",
                 "off": ""}}

  {"transcription": "$XDG_CONFIG_HOME/foot/theme.ini",
   "scripture": {"tome": "themes/{{aspect}}.ini"}, "seal": "0600"}
