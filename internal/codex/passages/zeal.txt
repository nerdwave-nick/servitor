Leave to overwrite what the servitor did not itself create

The servitor is cautious with the work of other hands, for it keeps no copy
of anything it casts down. A transcription replaces a vessel that stands
only when that vessel's scripture is the scripture of one of the rite's
aspects; a tether rebinds only a name that is already a tether. Anything else
standing in their way halts the pre-flight with heresy, and nothing at all
is performed.

Write "zeal": true upon a transcription or a tether to grant it leave to
sweep such obstacles aside: the transcription overwrites (or, for null
scripture, strikes) whatever vessel stands, and the tether casts down a
scripture-file standing where its name is to be bound. Even zeal never casts
down a hall.

Zeal is a truth for every aspect, never an aspect map. Use it knowingly: the
reversion of a falling invocation restores what a zealous step displaced,
but once the invocation triumphs, the former scripture is gone for ever.

Sanctums need no zeal, for they touch only their own warded region.

Kindred passages: transcription, tether, vessel, pre-flight

Exempla:
  {"transcription": "~/.config/foot/foot.ini", "zeal": true,
   "scripture": {"*": {"tome": "foot/{{aspect}}.ini"}}}

  {"tether": "~/.config/gtk-3.0/gtk.css", "zeal": true,
   "anchor": "~/.config/visages/{{aspect}}/gtk.css"}
