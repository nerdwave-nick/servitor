The heresies for which the pre-flight forbade an invocation, in binharic

When an invocation is spoken with the rune --binharic and its pre-flight
finds heresy, nothing is performed and no verdict is passed: the servitor
speaks upon stdout one object of binharic instead of the chronicle's line.
It bears the rite, the aspect it was to enter, and the key "forbidden": every
heresy the pre-flight found, one denunciation to each word, in the order of
the liturgy. An edict's forbiddance bears no aspect, for an edict enters
none.

"forbidden" is no verdict. The verdicts remain triumph, reverted and
faltered, and are passed only upon a liturgy that was performed; a forbidden
invocation touched nothing, wrote neither data-slate nor chronicle, and
earns the death-mark 2. The lament that names the heresies is spoken upon
stderr all the same, for the faithful who watch.

An unknown rite or aspect, words strayed after an edict's name or a
malformed rune are refused before the pre-flight is even begun: they too
bear the death-mark 2, yet speak only upon stderr, and stdout stays empty.

Kindred passages: binharic, pre-flight, heresy, death-marks, invoke

Exempla:
  servitor invoke theme porpl --binharic

  {"rite":"theme","aspect":"porpl","forbidden":["…/rites/theme.json:9:7: heresy: …"]}

  {"rite":"screenshot","forbidden":["…: heresy: the inscription \"region\" is mandatory, …"]}

  servitor invoke theme porpl --binharic | jq -r '.forbidden[]?'
