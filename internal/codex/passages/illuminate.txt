Filling the placeholders within a tome's scripture

Inline scripture is always illuminated. A tome is not: by default it is set
down exactly as it is written, its {{…}} marks untouched, for a tome may be
scripture of a tongue that uses such marks for its own ends. Write
"illuminate": true beside the tome to have its placeholders filled like any
inline scripture: {{aspect}}, {{former}}, {{rite.name}} and
{{inscription.<key>}}.

An illuminated tome is examined like the rite itself: an unknown
placeholder, an inscription the rite never declared, or a mark never closed
is heresy, reported at the tome's own line and column, and the invocation is
refused. Write {{{{ to set down a literal {{ in an illuminated tome.

"illuminate" is a truth, false unless written, and belongs only within a
tome object.

Kindred passages: tome, scripture, placeholders, inscriptions

Exempla:
  {"sanctum": "~/.config/mako/config",
   "scripture": {"tome": "mako/visage.ini", "illuminate": true}}

  The tome mako/visage.ini may then speak:
    background-color={{inscription.hue}}
    # visage: {{aspect}}, before it {{former}}
