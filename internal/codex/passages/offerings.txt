What is laid before a litany for its scroll to work with

A litany receives nothing it is not offered. Its "offerings" are a list of
words handed to the scroll in the order written, each as one whole word: the
servitor neither splits them at spaces, nor reads quotation marks, nor lets
any tongue interpret them, nor expands a leading "~" or a $NAME. Only their
placeholders are filled — {{aspect}}, {{former}}, {{rite.name}} and
{{inscription.<key>}} — so an inscription full of spaces and quotation marks
arrives at the scroll exactly as it was given.

Each offering may differ for every aspect as an aspect map; an aspect for
which an offering holds nothing, and no "*" serves, is heresy. Without
offerings the scroll is recited bare, and must learn what it needs from the
environment (SERVITOR_ASPECT and its kin).

Offerings belong to litanies alone. An incantation's words are handed whole
to its tongue instead, and need no offerings.

Kindred passages: litany, placeholders, aspect-maps, inscriptions

Exempla:
  {"litany": "scripts/change-theme",
   "offerings": ["{{aspect}}", "{{inscription.reason}}"]}

  {"litany": "scripts/screen-transition",
   "offerings": [{"night": "fade", "*": "wipe"}, "250"]}
