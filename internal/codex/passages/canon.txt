The inscriptions a rite declares, as the augury and the census speak them

Where "inscriptions" in the augury names the words the last invocation laid
upon the data-slate, "canon" names what the rite's scripture declares: every
inscription it may be given, in the order it is written. Other machines read
it to ask the faithful for each inscription before they invoke, without
reading the scripture themselves.

Each declaration of the canon bears five keys, always all of them:

  key         the inscription's name, spoken as the rune --<key>
  purpose     why it exists; "" when the scripture names none
  mandatory   true when every invocation must bear its word, from the
              rune or from a decree, else false
  decrees     the decree of every aspect that has one; a "*" of the
              scripture is resolved for each aspect it serves, and an
              aspect without a decree is absent, so no reader need ever
              interpret an aspect map
  decree      an edict's one plain decree, or null when it decrees nothing;
              always null for a rite, whose decrees stand per aspect

An edict knows no aspect, so its "decrees" is always {} and its decree, if
any, stands under "decree". A rite that declares no inscription bears
"canon": [], and so does a heretical rite, for nothing of its scripture is
kept.

The augury bears the canon beside "inscriptions", and every entry of the
census in binharic bears it too, so that a single census suffices to know
every rite and what each may be inscribed with.

Kindred passages: inscriptions, decrees, mandatory, purpose, augury,
census, binharic, edict, aspect-maps

Exempla:
  servitor augury theme | jq .canon

  "canon": [{"key": "reason", "purpose": "why the visage was changed",
             "mandatory": false, "decrees": {}, "decree": null}]

  "canon": [{"key": "mode", "purpose": "", "mandatory": true,
             "decrees": {"off": "always-visible", "on": "auto-hide"},
             "decree": null}]

  "canon": [{"key": "region", "purpose": "the slurp geometry",
             "mandatory": false, "decrees": {}, "decree": "full"}]

  servitor census --binharic |
    jq -r '.[] | .rite as $r | .canon[] | select(.mandatory) | "\($r) --\(.key)"'
