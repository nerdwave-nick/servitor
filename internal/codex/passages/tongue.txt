The program in which enact words, observances, verses and auspexes are spoken

The tongue is the language the servitor speaks its commands in: the program
to which a machine-spirit's enact words and auspex, an incantation's words
and a reversion are handed as "<tongue> -c <words>", and through which a
litany's scroll is recited when the scroll cannot be executed by its own
shebang. Unless commanded otherwise
the servitor speaks bash.

A tongue is named by one word alone: the name or path of a single program,
sought along the machine's PATH. No words may follow it — "bash -e" names a
program that does not exist. Should you need such words, speak them inside
the incantation itself ("set -e; …").

The tongue may be named in three places, and the nearest prevails: on a
machine-spirit (for its enact words and its auspex), an observance or a
verse; on the rite (for everything it speaks); and in the settings (for
every rite of the Librarium).
Where none is named, bash is spoken.

Before anything is performed, the pre-flight seeks every tongue the
invocation will speak; a tongue spoken nowhere on this machine refuses the
invocation, and the inquisition notes it as an impurity. A litany that is
recited by its own shebang needs no tongue, unless it carries a reversion.

The tongue of exalted speech is fixed when its master sanctions it, as the
program the tongue names from the root. The exalted servitor speaks it only
if that program is owned by the root, written by none but the root, and
reached through halls the root alone may write, and only if it is one of
the tongues exalted speech knows how to bid read no scripture of its own as
it awakens, judged by the last name of the program it truly is: bash, spoken
as "bash --noprofile --norc"; zsh, as "zsh -f"; fish, as "fish --no-config";
sh and dash as they are, for neither reads any when it is not interactive,
and ENV never reaches them. Any other tongue of exalted words is heresy,
and the exalted servitor refuses it; the pre-flight refuses it before any
proof is asked. Where /bin/sh is bash, sh is spoken as bash.

Kindred passages: incantation, litany, reversion, auspex, patience,
exalted-servitor

Exempla:
  // on an observance
  {"name": "herald", "designation": "incantation", "tongue": "zsh",
   "words": "print -P '%F{red}{{aspect}}%f'"}

  // on a rite, for everything it speaks
  {"pattern": "Mark II", "aspects": ["on", "off"], "tongue": "dash",
   "subjects": [{"name": "veil", "designation": "machine-spirit",
                 "auspex": {"scan": "test -e /run/user/1000/veiled && echo on || echo off"},
                 "enact": {"on": "touch /run/user/1000/veiled",
                           "off": "rm -f /run/user/1000/veiled"}}]}

  // in servitor.json, for the whole Librarium
  {"pattern": "Mark II", "tongue": "/usr/bin/fish"}
