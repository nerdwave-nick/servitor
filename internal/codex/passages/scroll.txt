The scroll an observance or an entry of an edict recites

Under "scroll" an observance or an edict's entry designated "litany" names
the scroll it recites: by its own shebang when the machine may execute it,
else through its tongue. A path that does not begin at the root is reckoned
from the hall of the rite's scripture. Its "offerings" are laid before it,
each written once and handed over exactly as written.

The scroll is named once for every aspect, never as an aspect map, and may
not be empty. Exalted, its words are sealed into the sanction, and the
scriptures it reads beside it are named among its "relics".

Kindred passages: litany, offerings, observances, edict, relics
