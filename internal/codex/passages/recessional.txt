The observances spoken after a subject changed

Under "recessional" a subject names the observances to be spoken after it
was set. Each named observance is spoken once in an invocation, just after
the last changing subject that names it, and only when its guards admit
the invocation; when no subject naming it changes, it is not spoken.

A name in "recessional" that names no declared observance is heresy, and so
is an observance named both as an introit and as a recessional. Should the
invocation fall, a spoken observance speaks its reversion, in reverse order
with everything else performed.

Kindred passages: introit, observances, subjects, to, from

Exempla:
  {"name": "mako", "designation": "machine-spirit",
   "omens": {"on": "do-not-disturb", "*": "default"},
   "auspex": {"scan": "makoctl mode"},
   "enact": {"*": "makoctl mode -s \"$SERVITOR_OMEN\""},
   "recessional": ["herald-silence"]}
