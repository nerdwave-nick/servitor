Speak in binharic, the tongue of machines, for other machines to read

The rune --binharic is given to the census and to the Inquisition. Their
answers are then rendered not as tables and sentences for the faithful but as
binharic for lesser machines: indented objects with stable keys, which other
commands may parse — jq foremost among them.

The census in binharic is a list in which every rite bears rite, aspect,
standing, aspects, purpose, desecrated, last_rite and recorded_in. The
Inquisition in binharic is a list of findings, each bearing scripture, line,
column, rite, judgement ("heresy" or "impurity") and denunciation; its exit
remains 1 when any heresy was found.

The augury needs no such rune: spoken without a key it always answers in
binharic, and spoken with a key it answers with that single value.

Kindred passages: census, inquisition, augury

Exempla:
  servitor census --binharic | jq -r '.[] | "\(.rite) \(.aspect)"'
  servitor inquisition --binharic | jq length
