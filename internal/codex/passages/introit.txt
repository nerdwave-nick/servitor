The observances spoken before a subject changes

Under "introit" a subject names the observances to be spoken before it is
set. Each named observance is spoken once in an invocation, just before the
first changing subject that names it, and only when its guards admit the
invocation; subjects passed over, already bearing their omen, call upon
nothing.

A name in "introit" that names no declared observance is heresy, and so is
an observance named both as an introit and as a recessional. Should the
invocation fall, a spoken observance speaks its reversion, in reverse order
with everything else performed.

Kindred passages: recessional, observances, subjects, to, from
