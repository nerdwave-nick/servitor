Withdraw the sanction of a rite's exalted verses, or every sanction, with one proof

A sanction stands until it is abjured. Spoken with a rite, the ritual
withdraws the sanction laid for that rite; spoken with --all, every sanction
laid for you. Its exalted verses are performed no more until they are
sanctioned anew, and the rite stands unsanctioned.

The intercessor asks for the master's proof, once, whatever is abjured, and
the exalted servitor it raises strikes each sanction from the store where
only the machine's own authority writes, and journals the master's uid, the
rite and the sigil of each. A forsaken sanction — whose rite is gone, or
holds no exalted verse — is abjured as any other, and needs no rite of the
Librarium. No terminal need attend: the intercessor chooses as for every
proof.

Each sanction struck is told upon stdout. A rite whose sanction is not laid,
--all when none is, or words that name no rite, two rites, or a rite beside
--all, are refused before any proof, the death-mark 2. A proof dismissed or
failed strikes nothing and bears 1; an intercessor nowhere to be found, a
servitor installed amiss, and whatever the exalted servitor refuses bear 2.
Roused as root the ritual is refused, its death-mark 2.

Kindred passages: sanction, all, sigil, exalted, intercessor, exalted-servitor

Exempla:
  servitor abjure split-lock
  servitor abjure --all
  servitor sanction --binharic | jq -r '.[] | select(.standing == "forsaken") | .rite' | xargs -rn1 servitor abjure
