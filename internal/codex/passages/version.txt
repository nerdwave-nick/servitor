Reveal the pattern of the servitor itself

The rune --version (shorthand -v) is given to the servitor alone, before any
ritual. The servitor then speaks the pattern it was forged in — the version
recorded when it was built, or "dev" for a servitor forged from unmarked
sources — with a blessing, and does nothing else.

Do not confuse the servitor's own pattern with the pattern of scripture: the
key "pattern" of a rite or of the settings names the form the scripture is
written in, "Mark I" for this servitor, while the rune --version names the
forging of the servitor itself.

Kindred passages: pattern, expound

Exempla:
  servitor --version
  servitor -v
