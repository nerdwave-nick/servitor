Read the aspect a rite stands in, how it stands, and its inscriptions

The augury reads the omens of one rite and speaks what they reveal: the
aspect the machine stands in, the rite's standing, whether other hands
desecrated it, the inscriptions of its last invocation, its last rite with
verdict and hour, every omen with its verse, and every taint upon its
sanctums. The augury changes nothing; it awakens the rite's auspex, should
the rite keep one, but performs no step.

Without a key, the augury speaks one object in binharic, for other machines
to read:

  {"rite": "theme", "aspect": "porpl", "standing": "performed",
   "desecrated": false, "inscriptions": {"reason": "gaming remnant"},
   "last_rite": {"verdict": "triumph", "at": "2026-10-10T13:30:00Z"},
   "omens": [{"verse": 2, "tether": "~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme",
              "aspect": "porpl"}, {"auspex": true, "aspect": "porpl"}],
   "taint": []}

The key "former", naming the aspect the data-slate recorded, appears only
when the rite is desecrated.

With a key, the augury speaks that single value and nothing more: aspect,
standing, desecrated, former, or any inscription the rite declares (an empty
line when it is uninscribed). Should an inscription bear the name of one of
the first four, the augury's own value is spoken; the inscription is still
read in the binharic object.

With the rune --is <aspect>, the augury speaks nothing at all: it exits 0
when the rite stands in that aspect and 1 when it does not, for the
conditions of other rites and commands.

The augury exits 0 when it was read — a dormant or heretical rite is still
read and spoken — 1 when --is did not match, and 2 when it could not be read:
an unknown rite or key, an aspect the rite knows not, a key given beside
--is, a heretical rite asked --is, or a malformed rune.

Kindred passages: is, omens, standing, data-slate, inscriptions, auspex

Exempla:
  servitor augury theme
  servitor augury theme aspect
  servitor augury theme reason
  servitor augury theme | jq -r .inscriptions.reason
  servitor augury mouse --is on && echo "the cursor is veiled"
