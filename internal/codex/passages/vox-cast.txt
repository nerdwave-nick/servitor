A step that sends word of the invocation's progress or triumph

A vox-cast is a step that does nothing to the machine and everything for its
master: it sends tidings of the invocation as it is performed. Its own key
holds which tidings to proclaim, and the codex knows but two:

  "progress"  tells of the kind of step performed last and how far the
              liturgy has come, counting only the steps that act upon the
              machine (a progress cast before any such step tells that the
              liturgy begins)
  "success"   proclaims the triumph of the rite and the aspect it now bears

Tidings of failure need no step: should a step fall, the servitor proclaims
the heresy, the fallen step and how the reversion went, unbidden. The words
themselves are drawn at random from the servitor's own litanies of progress,
triumph and lament, and speak of the aspect, written «aspect», and of the
step's own key; a rite does not write them.

Where the tidings are heard depends on whence the servitor was roused. With
a controlling terminal, each vox-cast is told in its place within the
report of the invocation (see invoke): a progress as a line of its own,
ending in the count of steps done, and the triumph as the report's closing
line. A fall is told by the lament alone.

    ✔ verse 2 · tether ~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme
    ⋯ Incense burns; the tether is complete.  (2/3)
    ✔ verse 4 · litany scripts/recite-hooks
  ✠ Binharic hymns resound: the aspect «porpl» is attained.

Without one — invoked from a hotkey or a launcher — every vox-cast of the
invocation becomes one missive upon the desktop through notify-send, headed
by the rite and its aspect ("theme → porpl") and replaced in place by the
next: progress tells "step x / n" and lingers until the rite ends, triumph
fades as the desktop wills, and a fall is proclaimed with critical urgency
so that it cannot pass unseen. Should notify-send be absent or fall silent,
the servitor stays silent too; a vox-cast never fails an invocation. The
settings' "vox" may send the vox-casts of a terminal's invocations to the
desktop as well ("notify-send"), or silence the desktop altogether ("off");
when foreseeing, nothing
is proclaimed at all.

A "success" vox-cast that is not the last step is an impurity, for it would
proclaim a triumph not yet won. Any other value is heresy.

Kindred passages: vox, liturgy, verdicts, invoke

Exempla:
  "liturgy": [
    {"vox-cast": "progress"},
    {"tether": "~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme",
     "anchor": "~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}"},
    {"vox-cast": "progress"},
    {"litany": "scripts/reload-theme"},
    {"vox-cast": "success"}
  ]
