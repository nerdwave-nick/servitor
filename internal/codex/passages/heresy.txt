The failings the inquisition denounces: heresies and impurities

Whatever is wrong with a rite, its settings or the machine it acts upon is
denounced as one of two judgements. A heresy must be purged: a rite in heresy
cannot be invoked, and its pre-flight refuses to begin. An impurity is noted
and forgiven: the rite may still be invoked, but the servitor bids the scribe
take heed.

Heresies of the scripture: malformed JSONC; unknown keys; a missing or foreign
pattern; aspects empty, repeated or unworthily named; a rite bearing both
"aspects" and "edict": true, or neither; aspect-map keys that name no declared
aspect, and an aspect left without value and without "*"; in an edict, a
sanctum, transcription or tether, an auspex, any aspect map, and the marks
{{aspect}} and {{former}}; unknown placeholders and undeclared inscriptions;
tomes that cannot be found; a sanctum whose scripture is null; a vox-cast that
is neither "progress" nor "success"; a ward claimed twice within one vessel;
two scriptures recorded for one rite; heretical settings; a vessel whose
sanctum markers are broken, which no invocation could rewrite; and the
heresies of exaltation — "exalted" upon a vox-cast or not true or false,
relics upon a verse that is not exalted, lying beyond the rite's hall or not
to be found, {{former}}
in exalted words, an exalted vessel not rooted or beneath /proc, /sys or
/dev, an exalted tether anchored in the invoker's halls, exalted speech in a
tongue the exalted servitor does not know, and an "intercessor" in the
settings that is none of auto, sudo, run0 and pkexec.

Impurities: a tongue spoken nowhere on this machine; a vessel missing whose
sanctum may not consecrate it; an anchor that stands nowhere for some aspect;
the scroll of an edict's litany that stands nowhere, for no step of an
edict can write it; a mandatory inscription without a decree for some
aspect; a "success" vox-cast that is not the last step; a tainted sanctum;
a desecrated rite; an exalted rite unsanctioned or lapsed, and a forsaken
sanction; sudo, run0, pkexec or doas in words that are not exalted; exalted
speech reaching into the invoker's home, or for scripture beside it that is
not among its relics; a program that asks a proof of its own (systemctl,
hostnamectl, …) in a verse that is not exalted beside exalted ones; and
/usr/bin/servitor missing or not the root's while a rite holds exalted
verses.

Every judgement carries its place — scripture:line:column — and a verbose
denunciation of what is amiss and how it may be purged. The inquisition
speaks them all and exits 1 when any heresy was found, 0 when only
impurities or nothing at all were. In binharic each judgement is one object
of "scripture", "line", "column", "rite" (absent for the settings and the
Librarium itself), "judgement" (heresy or impurity) and "denunciation".

A heresy found in the pre-flight of an invocation halts it before any step
is performed; heresies spoken there are the same the inquisition would speak.

Kindred passages: inquisition, pre-flight, standing, spare-vessels, binharic, exalted, sanction

Exempla:
  servitor inquisition
  servitor inquisition theme --binharic

  {"scripture": "/home/me/.config/servitor/rites/theme.json", "line": 7,
   "column": 5, "rite": "theme", "judgement": "heresy",
   "denunciation": "…"}
