The aspects an observance is spoken into

Under "to" an observance lists the declared aspects it is spoken into: an
invocation into any other aspect passes it over, though a subject naming it
changes. Without "to" an observance is spoken into every aspect.

Every aspect named must be declared by the rite, else heresy. Words that
differ by aspect are written as several observances, each guarded by "to",
for the words of an observance are never an aspect map.

Kindred passages: from, observances, introit, recessional, aspects

Exempla:
  {"name": "herald-silence", "designation": "incantation", "to": ["on"],
   "words": "notify-send 'The heralds fall silent'"}

  {"name": "herald-wakes", "designation": "incantation", "to": ["off"],
   "words": "notify-send 'The heralds speak again'"}
