A named scripture that brings the machine into an aspect, or proclaims an edict

A rite is the servitor's unit of devotion: one scripture-file in the rites/
hall of the Librarium, written in JSONC — JSON that suffers comments and
trailing commas — and bearing "pattern": "Mark II". The rite is named after
its scripture-file without the extension: rites/theme.json (or theme.jsonc)
is the rite "theme". Only the rites/ hall is read; its halls and every name
beginning with "." are passed over.

A rite's name must begin with a letter or digit and continue only with
letters, digits, ".", "_" or "-". Two scriptures for one name — theme.json
beside theme.jsonc — is heresy, and neither is believed. Every key of the
scripture is examined, and a key the codex does not know is heresy, so a
misspelt key never passes in silence.

A rite is of one of two kinds. A rite of aspects declares its "aspects" and
its "subjects": every thing of the machine it reads and sets, each by
itself, in written order, and the "observances" spoken when they change. It
keeps no liturgy and no auspex of its own: each subject bears its own omens,
and a machine-spirit its own auspex. An edict declares "edict": true in
place of aspects, and keeps a "liturgy" of incantations and litanies,
proclaimed whole each time it is invoked; it bears no subjects and no
observances.

The keys of a rite are "pattern", "purpose", "aspects" or "edict",
"inscriptions", "tongue", "vox-cast", and "subjects" and "observances" for a
rite of aspects or "liturgy" for an edict; only the pattern is demanded,
and beside it exactly one of "aspects" and "edict": true. A "liturgy" or an
"auspex" upon a rite of aspects is heresy, and the words name where its
deeds now dwell: its subjects, the auspex of a machine-spirit, its
observances. A heretical rite is shown in the census and the cogitator
with its heresies, but it cannot be invoked until it is purified.

A rite of pattern "Mark I" is converted to Mark II on its first reading
(see conversion).

Each rite becomes a ritual of its own beneath invoke, with its aspects (an
edict has none) for completion and its inscriptions as runes; {{rite.name}}
illuminates as its name, and its words receive it as SERVITOR_RITE.

Kindred passages: pattern, aspects, edict, subjects, observances, liturgy,
inscriptions, conversion, librarium

Exempla:
  // <Librarium>/rites/mouse-autohide-toggle.json
  {
    "pattern": "Mark II",
    "purpose": "Hide the cursor after 400ms of stillness",
    "aspects": ["on", "off"],
    "vox-cast": ["success"],
    "subjects": [
      {"name": "cursor", "designation": "sanctum",
       "vessel": "~/.config/niri/util.kdl", "ward": "mouse-autohide",
       "omens": {"on": "cursor {\n    hide-after-inactive-ms 400\n}", "off": ""},
       "recessional": ["reread"]},
    ],
    "observances": [
      {"name": "reread", "designation": "incantation",
       "words": "niri msg action load-config-file"},
    ],
  }

  servitor invoke mouse-autohide-toggle on
