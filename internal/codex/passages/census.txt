Take a census of every rite of the Librarium and how each stands

The census reads every rite kept in the Librarium and names, for each, the
aspect it stands in, its standing — performed, dormant, corrupted, tainted,
desecrated or heretical — its aspects, its last rite with verdict and hour,
and its purpose. A rite that stands corrupted or tainted and was also
desecrated is named with both. The census reads the omens of every rite and
awakens each auspex, but performs nothing.

Spoken plainly, the census is a table with the columns RITE, ASPECT,
STANDING, ASPECTS, LAST RITE and PURPOSE; a "·" marks what is not known. A
Librarium without rites is told so, with the counsel to consecrate one.

With --binharic, the census is a list for other machines, and every rite
bears the keys rite, aspect, standing, aspects, purpose, desecrated,
last_rite and recorded_in — the last naming the scripture the rite was read
from. A rite whose data-slate cannot be read is still counted; the lament is
spoken apart from the census.

Kindred passages: augury, standing, binharic, librarium, inquisition

Exempla:
  servitor census
  servitor census --binharic |
    jq -r '.[] | select(.standing != "performed") | .rite'
  servitor --librarium ~/dotfiles/servitor census
