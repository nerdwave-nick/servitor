The form-codex of a rite and of the settings, for the editors of the faithful

The servitor bears two schemas of pattern Mark I, written in JSON Schema, the
common tongue of form that the machine spirits of many editors understand:
one for the scripture of a rite, one for the settings in servitor.json. An
editor that knows them offers every key as it is written, names the forms
each key permits, recites the lore of each key beside it, and denounces a
malformed key before the Inquisition ever convenes.

A scripture names its schema under "$schema", by custom its first key. The
servitor accepts "$schema" in every rite and in the settings and heeds it
not: it changes nothing of an invocation, and the Inquisition asks only that
it be written as a string. Should the servitor write a rite's scripture
anew, its "$schema" is kept, first as before.

The schemas are kept in the servitor's own repository and are named by their
raw address:

  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nerdwave-nick/servitor/main/schema/rite.schema.json
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nerdwave-nick/servitor/main/schema/settings.schema.json

The rune --schema bids expound recite a schema itself rather than a passage:
the schema of a rite when spoken alone or with "rite", the schema of the
settings when spoken with "settings". What is recited is the very schema kept
in the repository, letter for letter, to be kept beside the Librarium where
no noosphere reaches.

A schema knows the form of every key, but not the rite it stands in: it
cannot tell whether an aspect map names only declared aspects and serves
them all, whether a placeholder is known, or whether a tome stands where it
is named. The Inquisition alone judges these.

Kindred passages: expound, inquisition, rite, pattern, aspect-maps

Exempla:
  {"$schema": "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nerdwave-nick/servitor/main/schema/rite.schema.json",
   "pattern": "Mark I",
   "aspects": ["on", "off"]}

  servitor expound --schema > ~/.config/servitor/rite.schema.json
  servitor expound --schema settings
