Where a tether leads: the far end of the binding for each aspect

The anchor is the place a tether's name leads to. A tether subject writes
its anchors as its "omens": an object naming the anchor of each aspect,
with "*" serving the rest. Written null for an aspect, the anchor decrees
that the name shall be unbound when that aspect is invoked. The key
"anchor" itself belongs to the elder pattern "Mark I", where it stood beside
the tether step, one place for every aspect or an aspect map; conversion
makes it the omens of the tether subject.

The anchor may speak placeholders, so one anchor may serve every aspect:
"~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}". A leading "~" and $NAMES are expanded,
and an anchor not rooted at "/" is reckoned from the rites/ hall of the
Librarium; the tether is bound to the full path so reckoned. Inscriptions
may appear in an anchor; the augury then renders it with the inscriptions
the data-slate keeps.

The anchor need not stand for the tether to be bound, yet the inquisition
notes as impurity an anchor that stands nowhere for some aspect, for a name
leading into the void serves no one.

The anchor is the tether's omen: the augury reads where the name leads, and
every aspect whose rendered anchor matches it is an aspect the tether fits.
An unbound name fits every aspect whose anchor is null.

Kindred passages: tether, omens, aspect-maps, placeholders, zeal, conversion

Exempla:
  {"name": "visage", "designation": "tether",
   "tether": "~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme",
   "omens": {"*": "~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}"}}

  {"name": "shaders", "designation": "tether", "tether": "~/.config/mpv/shaders",
   "omens": {"crisp": "~/.local/share/shaders/crisp", "*": null}}

  // pattern Mark I, before conversion
  {"tether": "~/.config/mpv/shaders",
   "anchor": {"crisp": "~/.local/share/shaders/crisp", "*": null}}
