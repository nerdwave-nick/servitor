Where a tether leads: the far end of the binding for each aspect

The anchor is the place a tether's name leads to. It is written under
"anchor" beside the tether, either one place for every aspect or an aspect
map naming the anchor of each aspect, with "*" serving the rest. Written
null for an aspect, the anchor decrees that the name shall be unbound when
that aspect is invoked.

The anchor may speak placeholders, so one anchor may serve every aspect:
"~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}". A leading "~" and $NAMES are expanded,
and an anchor not rooted at "/" is reckoned from the rites/ hall of the
Librarium; the tether is bound to the full path so reckoned. Inscriptions
may appear in an anchor; the augury then renders it with the inscriptions
the data-slate keeps.

The anchor need not stand for the tether to be bound, yet the inquisition
notes as impurity an anchor that stands nowhere for some aspect, for a name
leading into the void serves no one.

The anchor is the tether's omen: the augury reads where the name leads, and
the aspect whose rendered anchor matches it is the aspect the tether bears
witness to. An unbound name is the omen of the aspect whose anchor is null.

Kindred passages: tether, aspect-maps, placeholders, omens, zeal

Exempla:
  {"tether": "~/.local/share/nfluff/current-theme",
   "anchor": "~/.config/nfluff/themes/{{aspect}}"}

  {"tether": "~/.config/mpv/shaders",
   "anchor": {"crisp": "~/.local/share/shaders/crisp", "*": null}}
