The hall where rites and settings are kept, and the rune naming it

The Librarium is the servitor's archive: a hall holding the hall rites/, in
which every rite is recorded as one scripture (<name>.json or <name>.jsonc,
the name of the scripture-file being the rite's name), and beside it the
settings in servitor.json. Only rites/ is read; scriptures kept elsewhere in
the Librarium are not rites.

The servitor finds its Librarium thus, the first that speaks prevailing: the
universal rune --librarium (shorthand -l), given to any ritual; then the
environment's SERVITOR_LIBRARIUM; then $XDG_CONFIG_HOME/servitor, or
~/.config/servitor when that is not set. A leading "~" and the $NAMES of the
environment are expanded, and a relative path is reckoned from the hall the
servitor was summoned in. Should the rune be spoken more than once, the last
prevails.

Roused as root by sudo, the servitor seeks the Librarium of the one who spoke
sudo, never root's own: SUDO_USER names them, and their home is SUDO_HOME,
else the home the machine records for SUDO_USER. Root's $XDG_CONFIG_HOME,
$XDG_STATE_HOME and every other XDG_ variable are cast away and "~" is that
home, so that the settings, the vessels, the data-slates and the chronicle are
the invoker's too. Should that home not be found, the servitor reads nothing
and bears the death-mark 2. Without SUDO_USER, root's own Librarium is read.
As root the servitor only reads — foresight, augury, census, inquisition and
expound — and writes nothing; every invocation, and the cogitator, is refused.

Naming another Librarium changes everything at once: its rites, its settings
and — where its settings name one — its chronicle.

Paths within a rite that do not begin at the root — vessels, tethers,
anchors, tomes and litany scrolls — are reckoned from the hall rites/ of the
Librarium, where the rite's scripture is kept, and incantations, litanies and
the auspex are spoken in that hall.

Kindred passages: rite, chronicle, pattern, vessel, tongue, patience, vox, intercessor

Exempla:
  servitor --librarium ~/dotfiles/servitor census
  servitor -l ./sandbox invoke theme porpl --foresee
  SERVITOR_LIBRARIUM=~/dotfiles/servitor servitor cogitator
